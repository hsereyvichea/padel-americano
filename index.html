    Individual scoring

    Each game has a fixed number of total points. If Team A scores 14 in a 24-point match, Team B scores 10. Both teammates earn their team's points — you play for yourself, whoever your partner is.

    Team A · 14Team B · 10
    +14 → each A player+10 → each B player

    Highest individual total at the end wins. Draws are allowed — 12–12 in a 24-point match gives everyone 12.

    Rotating partners

    Every round you get a new partner and new opponents. With 4, 8, 12 or 16 players a full rotation is mathematically perfect — you partner every other player exactly once.

    R1Alex & BeavsCarlos & Dana
    R2Alex & CarlosvsBea & Dana
    R3Alex & DanavsBea & Carlos

    With 5, 9 or 13 players one person sits out each round; other counts rotate byes and minimise repeat partners automatically.

    Formats & pace

    • Full rotation — the complete partner cycle (e.g. 7 rounds for 8 players, 15 for 16).
    • Quick format — six rounds, social pace, still no repeat partners where possible.
    • 16 points — fast games, big field, quick night.
    • 24 points — the classic Americano distance.
    • 32 points — long games, more rallies, fitter heads.

    One or two courts

    • 1 court · sequential — matches of a round play one after another.
    • 2 courts · simultaneous — two matches at once; needs at least 8 players. Extra matches queue as a second wave.
    • Switching court count keeps every recorded score — only the court labels change.

    Winning & tiebreaks

    The champion is the player with the highest individual points total after the last round — not the pair that won the most games.

    • Equal points? Point difference (scored minus conceded) decides.
    • Still equal? Match wins, then alphabetical order.
    • Finished early? The podium locks in and the champion is crowned automatically.
